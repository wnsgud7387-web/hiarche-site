Why "Arche"?

An old Greek word for beginning, and six apps built around one.

Made by hand,
one stitch at a time.

Arche is a small studio in Korea that makes apps by hand, one careful stitch at a time. Nothing flashy, just well-made tools you'll want to keep using.

Our name comes from the Greek arche, "beginning." Every app is built for a first step: the first page, the first walk, the first day.
