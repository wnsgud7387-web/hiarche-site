A new habit app, the night of October 4

Arche, a one-person company building apps with an AI team · Part 5

Day 63 (as of Nov 17) · What didn't work: No. 3

As I wrote in Part 4, the numbers in early October were quiet. One subscriber. Three or four people a day using the app.

Around then, I started one more app.

October 4, 11 p.m.

The new app's first name was "Tomorrow's Me." It's an app that helps with habits.

Three names

That same day, "Tomorrow's Me" changed names twice more: first "Small Wins – Microsuccess," then "Chosim – Small Win Routine." Chosim is Korean for "the first mind," the way you felt when you began.

People nobody called

Chosim came out on the App Store in early October.

On October 12, six people a day found Chosim on their own, with no ads. Not a big number. But nobody had called them.

What didn't work, No. 3

Google Play. On October 26 the Android version of Wanderlure (Holin) was rejected, and early the next morning the Android version of Chosim was rejected too. Chosim's reason: "app not responding."

In between, on the night of October 26, the company got a new rule. Before anything goes out, launch it on real devices. One new device, one old one. It ships only if neither crashes even once.

Small wins

An app meant to help with small wins got small numbers first.

When you start something new, how do you hold on to how you felt at the beginning?

Next: October 13, "overseas first."
