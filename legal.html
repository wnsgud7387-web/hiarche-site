This website and the LQ test

This site uses no cookies or third-party analytics. LQ results are calculated only in your browser. Only if you agree and press Send on the result screen is your LQ record (LQ and area scores, right/wrong and time per item, language, device type, date sent, how you arrived, and age band and gender if you chose them) sent to Arche's storage (Supabase Inc., servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea, acting as our processor). When you open this site, our hosting provider Vercel Inc. (USA) may process access logs (IP address, access time, browser information) for security and operation, in the US or other countries, for the period set by Vercel's own policy. Our storage provider Supabase may also keep access logs, including IP addresses, for 1 day for security. Arche does not receive these logs or link them to LQ records. If you do not want this processing abroad, please do not visit this site. We count how far people get in the test (started, stopped midway, finished, retook) without names or device IDs, to improve the test. No scores or answers are included, and counts are deleted after 800 days (about 2 years). If you press 'Do not send step counts on this visit' at the bottom of the test page, or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, nothing is counted. Contact: Arche privacy team · archeisit@gmail.com
