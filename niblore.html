Niblore

Write a web novel one episode at a time with AI.

Give it one line of a hint and get the next episode. Or skip writing and just read a story made for you.

Available onApp StoreAvailable onGoogle Play

Free to start · iPhone, iPad and Android · English, Korean, Japanese

What you can do

One hint, one episode

Type what should happen next. Niblore writes the episode and keeps your characters straight.

Just read

Pick a genre and a starting story, then read episode after episode.

Illustrations & sharing

Add a picture to a scene and share your story with a link.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Free to start. Ink packs and a monthly plan let you write and illustrate more. Prices are shown in the App Store or Google Play in your currency.
