Solomon's Verdict

Two sides of an argument, one AI judge.

Each of you writes your side on your own phone. Solomon, an AI judge, questions both of you in turn and rules with a fault percentage.

App Store · in reviewGoogle Play · soon

iPhone · In App Store review · Korean (English coming) · Ages 14+

What you can do

File and summon

Write what happened, then send a link. The other person answers on their own phone.

A real back-and-forth

Solomon asks each side questions before it decides, so both stories are heard.

Verdict, jury and a promise

Fault percentages by issue, an optional friend jury, and a few rules so the same fight does not happen again.
