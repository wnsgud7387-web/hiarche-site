Story Accounting

Short accounting stories for the FA, MA and FR exams.

Each story stops right before the answer, so you make the call. Get it wrong and it tells you the name of the mistake.

What you can do

Learn by story

Standards explained through short scenes, with the worked problem beside each one.

Your turn

The story pauses before the answer and asks you to choose.

A mistake log

Wrong answers are named and saved, so you can see your patterns.
