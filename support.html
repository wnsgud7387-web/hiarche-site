Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app's settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store: Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Each app's privacy policy explains what it keeps and how to delete it. Still unsure? Email us.
